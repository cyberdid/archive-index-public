# Licensing and attribution

The author selected the same licensing scheme as the two supplied earlier
articles on 23 October 2026: article/experimental records under CC BY 4.0 and
executable source under Apache-2.0. The most recent article explicitly uses
this split; both prior Zenodo records identify their articles as CC BY 4.0.

| Material | License and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Original executable source, tests, configuration and general software documentation | Apache License 2.0; root `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` |
| Article text, LaTeX prose, PDF, original figures/captions, bibliography and publication documentation in `paper/manuscript/` | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; `paper/manuscript/LICENSE` |
| Original public-benchmark experimental summaries, assessments and records, including `paper/results/`, UA-PAR query/run/assessment records and exported field aggregates | CC BY 4.0 to the extent copyright/database rights apply; `paper/results/LICENSE` |
| Standalone executable code in manuscript/research directories | Apache-2.0, overriding surrounding prose/data defaults |
| Third-party corpus files, embedded notices, dependencies, fonts, model weights and referenced publications | Their own terms; no relicensing or ownership is asserted |

For CC BY material, attribute Oleksandr Pylypenko, identify the work and source
revision, link the license and indicate modifications. Preserve applicable
Apache notices for software. The complete license texts provide the legal
terms; this guide identifies their scope.

Private archive documents, raw field-study queries and other non-exported
private inputs are excluded from the publication license grant.

Corpus source metadata, snippets and third-party material embedded in records
retain their original terms. Public corpus redistribution still requires the
separate attribution/scope review in `paper/corpus/LICENSES.md`. Selecting the
licenses does not publish the repository or approve scientific declarations.

Earlier immutable candidate-006 and review packages remain unchanged. Their
historical notices predate this decision; the newly prepared licensed edition
contains the license files. No DOI or Zenodo publication is assigned here.
